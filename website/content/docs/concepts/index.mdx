Two nouns and one split carry the whole library. Read the first three pages in order; the rest are
behaviours you opt into when you need them.

## The model

- **[Notifiables](/docs/concepts/notifiables)** — anything that can receive a notification. Per-channel
  addresses come from `@RouteFor` decorators, and `@NotifiableId` marks the id.
- **[Notifications](/docs/concepts/notifications)** — a plain class. Annotate each payload method with
  its channel handle (`@Mail()`, `@Database()`) and `via()` is inferred for you.
- **[Channels & dispatchers](/docs/concepts/channels-and-dispatchers)** — the split everything else
  hangs off: a channel decides *how* a notification leaves, a dispatcher decides *where and when* it
  runs.

## Delivery

- **[Async dispatch](/docs/concepts/async-dispatch)** — what survives the trip to a worker: a
  notification serializes to `{ name, data }`, a notifiable to a reference.
- **[Fallback chains](/docs/concepts/fallback-chains)** — an ordered chain of channels, stopping at the
  first that reaches the recipient.
- **[Dispatch guards](/docs/concepts/dispatch-guards)** — dedup and throttle *before* any channel runs.

## Per-recipient concerns

- **[Multi-tenancy](/docs/concepts/multi-tenancy)** — the same user in many workspaces, each with its
  own feed; scope one send or fan out with `forTenant`.
- **[Localization](/docs/concepts/localization)** — resolve a locale off the notifiable and translate
  per recipient.
- **[Context capture](/docs/concepts/context-capture)** — who triggered this, in which tenant, under
  which trace, threaded through the lifecycle events.
